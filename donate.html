Restorative Giving

How Your Support Transforms Lives

At FKND Foundation Learning Centre, every child's story is sacred. We do not set fixed prices on hope or learning. Whether you are an individual sponsor, an organization, a church group, or an international friend, we invite you to contact us directly so we can coordinate your gift according to where it will have the greatest impact.

Child Tuition & Full Scholarships

Ensure bright, motivated learners facing severe family hardship remain in the classroom without disruption, with tuition fees, exam costs, and academic coaching fully covered.

Daily Wholesome Meals & Nutrition

Support our school meal program providing warm, nutrient-dense lunches and pure drinking water every single day, so no child is forced to study hungry.

Uniforms, Bags & Essential Learning Kits

Provide learners with dignified custom green school uniforms, sturdy backpacks, textbooks, exercise books, geometry sets, and writing instruments.

STEM, Coding & Digital Innovation

Empower rural Ghanaian students with modern computers, coding robotics kits, hands-on science experiment apparatus, and library reading books.

Campus Facilities & Classroom Expansion

Help expand modern classrooms, clean sanitation facilities, solar power lighting, and restorative recreation grounds for our expanding student body.

In-Kind & Material Donations

We gratefully welcome in-kind gifts such as desktop computers, laptops, library storybooks, sports equipment, musical instruments, and healthcare kits.
