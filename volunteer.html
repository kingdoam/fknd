Restorative Community Service

Why Volunteer at FKND Foundation?

At FKND Foundation Learning Centre, we believe education is the greatest restorative force on earth. Whether you are an educator, university student, artisan, healthcare worker, or professional, your presence ignites possibility.

Academic Tutoring & Literacy

Partner with classroom educators to help primary and junior high learners master reading, phonetic pronunciation, storytelling, and foundational mathematics.

Role: Classroom Assistant • 2-4 hrs/wk

STEM & Digital Innovation

Introduce students to computers, block-based coding, science fair experiments, and robotics. Empower rural learners with 21st-century technological tools.

Role: Tech Instructor • Flexible Scheduling

Creative Arts & Music

Lead creative workshops in traditional Ghanaian drumming, drawing, choir, spoken word, and drama to ignite children’s imagination and self-confidence.

Role: Arts Workshop Lead • After-school

Athletics & Physical Fitness

Coach school football teams, organize track & field training, teach sportsmanship, and help our learners develop teamwork, grit, and physical health.

Role: Sports Coach • Tuesdays & Thursdays

Campus Greening & Tree Planting

In honor of our emblem “Hope for a Cut Down Tree,” lead tree planting days, tend to our campus school garden, and teach children environmental stewardship.

Role: Sustainability Champion • Weekend Projects

Virtual & International Mentorship

Can’t visit Mfensi in person? Support our educators online, host video Q&A sessions, review student essays, or provide teacher training from anywhere in the world.

Role: Remote Mentor • 1 hr/bi-weekly
