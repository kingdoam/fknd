Step-by-Step Pathway

How to Apply to FKND

A transparent, welcoming five-step process from initial inquiry to your child's first day in class.

01

Eligibility & Inquiry

Review age criteria, curriculum focus, and grade pathways suitable for your child.

02

Online Application

Fill out our quick registration form with student background and contact details.

03

Documentation

Submit copy of birth certificate, previous school records, and immunization history.

04

Diagnostic Meeting

Join our educators for a friendly diagnostic assessment and parent conversation.

05

Enrollment & Welcome

Receive official admission confirmation, uniform fitting, and term orientation pack.

Enrolment Dossier

Required Application Documents

Please prepare the following items when completing your child's registration dossier:

Birth Certificate

Certified copy or passport copy for student age verification.

2 Passport Photos

Recent colored passport-size photographs of the student.

Academic Records

Terminal reports or transcript from the last school attended.

Health Record

Immunization card and emergency contact information.

Document Advisory: Clear photocopies or digital scans are acceptable during the initial registration process. Original physical certificates will be verified during the student's campus orientation.
Helpful Answers

Admissions FAQ

Quick answers for prospective parents and guardians.

We operate rolling admissions throughout the year for available classroom spaces. We strongly encourage families to complete registration at least 6 weeks before each term start date.
To guarantee individual attention and moral mentorship, our class sizes are strictly capped at 1:12 in Early Childhood and a maximum of 1:18 in Primary and Secondary classrooms.
Yes, we warmly encourage campus visits! Guided tours run Monday through Friday during administrative hours. You can reserve your visit date online using our "Book a Tour" tool or by calling our office.
Our assessment is a relaxed, supportive diagnostic evaluation designed to understand each learner's reading comprehension, foundational numeracy, and personal interests so our teachers can customize their learning path.
